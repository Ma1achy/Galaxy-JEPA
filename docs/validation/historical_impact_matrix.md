# Historical Impact Matrix

This matrix records the status of every major historical empirical claim and instrument in Galaxy-JEPA after the remediation and validation audit. No historical encoder checkpoints (`runs/m_v2/encoder.pt`, `runs/ladder_v2*`), historical outputs (`artifacts/out/`), or SHA-1 pinned scripts (`artifacts/dd_part4.py`, `artifacts/dd_part4b.py`) have been overwritten.

**Status vocabulary:**
- `UNCHANGED` — unaffected by any confirmed defect; existing evidence stands.
- `QUALIFIED` — held-out measurement remains informative, but scope/interpretation must be qualified (e.g. JEPA variant semantics, descriptive rather than causal/MI).
- `SUPERSEDED` — defect corrected and superseded by a clean erratum or replacement artefact.
- `RERUN_REQUIRED` — instrument fixed in code, but updated catalogue/model outputs require an authorised rerun (see [`rerun_runbook.md`](rerun_runbook.md)).
- `UNRESOLVED` — depends on future factorial ablation or unavailable hardware/data.
